# On Walking

There is a particular kind of thinking that only happens on foot.

At a desk, ideas line up and wait their turn. On a walk they arrive *sideways*: a half-remembered sentence, the shape of a problem seen from a new angle, the answer to a question you stopped asking an hour ago.

## Why it works

- The body is busy, so the mind wanders.
- Nothing asks for your attention.
- The scenery changes slowly enough to notice.

> Solvitur ambulando. It is solved by walking.

Next time you're stuck, leave the screen behind. Take the long way.
