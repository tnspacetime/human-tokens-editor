# Kitchen Notes: Weeknight Pasta

A fast dinner for two, about twenty minutes.

## Ingredients

- 200 g spaghetti
- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 4 tbsp olive oil
- A pinch of chilli flakes
- Parsley and lemon to finish

## Method

1. Boil the pasta in well-salted water.
2. Warm the oil and garlic gently until golden.
3. Add chilli, then a ladle of pasta water.
4. Toss the drained pasta in the pan until glossy.

| Step | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Pasta | 10 min |
| Sauce | 5 min |
| Toss | 1 min |

Ratio check: about $\frac{1}{2}$ ladle of water per 100 g of pasta.
