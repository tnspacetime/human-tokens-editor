# Slow Software

Every tool I love has one thing in common: it waits for me.

It doesn't guess what I meant, finish my sentence, or suggest a better one. It holds still while I think, and when I'm ready it does exactly what I asked. That stillness is a feature. It is the room in which a thought gets finished.

## The cost of help

Help that arrives before you ask for it changes what you were going to say. Sometimes for the better. Often just toward the middle — the most likely next word, the most common shape of an argument.

## What I want instead

A blank page that stays blank until I fill it. Undo that goes back far enough. A preview that shows me what I wrote, not what I might have written.
