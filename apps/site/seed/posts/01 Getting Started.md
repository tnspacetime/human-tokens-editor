# Getting Started with Swift

Swift is a **fast**, *modern* language for building apps on every Apple platform. This series walks through the ideas you'll use every day.

## What you need

- A Mac with Xcode installed
- About twenty minutes per part
- Curiosity

## Your first line

```swift
print("Hello, world!")
```

That's a complete program. No semicolons, no `main` function, no ceremony.

> The best way to learn a language is to write small things in it, often.
