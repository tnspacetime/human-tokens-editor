# Why I Write by Hand First

My first drafts happen on paper, in pencil, mostly because I can't search them.

When I can't search, I have to remember, and what I remember is usually what mattered. The rest — the clever phrase I was proud of for an hour, the tangent about fonts — doesn't survive the copy into the editor. Typing the draft up is the first edit.

It's slow. That's the point. By the time a sentence has been written twice, I believe it, or I've cut it.

> The pencil is a filter, not a tool.

I don't think everyone should do this. But if your writing feels like it's coming out too easily, try making it harder for a week.
